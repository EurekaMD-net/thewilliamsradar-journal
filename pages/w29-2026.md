## The Ticker of the Week — Deep Dive

> 🟡 ANALYST COMMENTARY — Editorial interpretation. Not model output.

**CMCSA — The Patient Trade Finally Escalates**

CMCSA has been in the radar for five consecutive weeks. It entered at S1 in W25 ($24.29), held through W26, W27, W28, and escalated to S2D this week at $23.97. Price is down from entry, AO is deeply negative (-3.88), but the AC just crossed above zero — the first sign of accumulation after five weeks of grinding lower.

The structural case hasn't changed: Comcast trades at 7.3x forward earnings for a business generating $17B in annual free cash flow. The SpinCo announcement (planned separation of NBCUniversal cable networks) removes the content drag from the valuation. The market has priced this as a terminal decline story; the model is reading it as a compression extreme with early accumulation.

What makes this week different from the previous four: the AC crossing above zero while price was actually up +1.7% this week — that's not a false technical cross. Price moved and accumulation confirmed simultaneously. That's the cleanest S2D entry signal the radar produces.

The risk: the SpinCo execution is a multi-year process and the broadband subscriber trend remains under pressure from fixed wireless competition (T-Mobile, Verizon). This is not a quick recovery trade. The model's S2D signal says accumulation is starting; it doesn't say the fundamental thesis resolves next quarter.

At p7% (price in the bottom 7% of its 52-week range), the setup is asymmetric. Second consecutive S2D week would be the confirmation trigger.
